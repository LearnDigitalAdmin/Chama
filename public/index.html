Two ways to run a loan — you choose per product.

Flat rate

Interest on the original amount

Interest is calculated once on the full principal for the whole term, then split evenly. Simple to explain at a meeting.

Example: KES 20,000 · 3 months · 5%/mo flat → interest KES 3,000 → KES 7,667/month
Reducing balance

Interest on what's still owed

Each installment's interest is calculated on the remaining balance, so interest falls as the loan is paid down.

Example: KES 20,000 · 6 months · 12% p.a. → installment ≈ KES 3,451/month

Terms & conditions

Demo copy — for illustration only, not legal advice.

1. The service. MyChama is provided by Samuhia Businesses ("Samuhia") to help chamas manage members, contributions, loans, communication and records. MyChama is a management tool; the funds and decisions of your chama remain your group's own responsibility.

2. Accounts and roles. Each chama designates a chair, treasurer and secretary as administrators. Administrators are responsible for the accuracy of records entered on behalf of the group and for approving loan disbursements.

3. Payments. Contributions, loan disbursements and repayments processed through MyChama are handled via Paystack. Samuhia is not a bank; settlement timing depends on your chosen settlement schedule and Paystack's processing times.

4. Messaging. Bulk SMS is sent one-way from SAMUHIA, subject to fair-use limits, available SMS credit balance, and an active internet connection at the time of sending. Delivery is currently supported to Safaricom numbers only.

5. Plans and billing. Paid plans renew automatically unless cancelled, and downgrade automatically to the Free plan on expiry if not renewed. SMS credits are billed separately from your plan.

6. Data. Your chama's records belong to your chama. Samuhia stores them to provide the service and does not sell member data.

7. Changes. These terms may be updated from time to time; continued use of MyChama after a change constitutes acceptance.

Privacy policy

Demo copy — for illustration only, not legal advice.

What we collect. Names, phone numbers and ID details for members you add; contribution and loan records your officials create; and messages sent through the platform.

Why. To run your chama's ledger, process payments through Paystack, send SMS notifications (shown to members as coming from SAMUHIA), and generate your exports and minutes.

Who can see it. Only your chama's own admins and, for their own records, individual members. Samuhia staff can access records only to provide support or comply with the law.

Sharing. We share the minimum data needed with Paystack (payments) and HostPinnacle (SMS delivery) to operate those features. We do not sell member data to third parties.

Your rights. Members can ask their chama's admin to correct or remove their details, subject to the group's own record-keeping obligations.
